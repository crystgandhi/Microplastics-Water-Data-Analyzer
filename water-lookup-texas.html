Texas microplastic study records

Galveston Bay tributaries

Sampling period and coverage
June 2020 to September 2021. Twenty surface-water samples from 10 tributary sites under baseflow and runoff conditions. Individual sampling dates and station identifiers: Not verified.
Reported concentration
0.02–6.74 particles/m³ among detections in the >1,000–4,760 µm fraction. This fraction was detected in 18 of 20 samples. The highest reported concentration occurred during stormflow at Clear Creek near Friendswood.
Collection and identification
Boat-towed net and Raman spectroscopy. Analyzed particle sizes: 300–4,760 µm. Net mesh and blank corrections: Not verified.
Sample-level particle counts and filtered volumes
Not verified.
Great Lakes Experiment 5 comparability
The reported concentration range applies to particles larger than 1,000 µm. Counts for 355–1,000 µm and corresponding filtered volumes need verification before using this dataset for model validation.
